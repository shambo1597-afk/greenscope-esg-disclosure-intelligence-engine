## Metric definitions

- **Hit@k**: share of questions where at least one of the top k chunks comes from a gold page.
- **MRR@5**: average of 1/rank of the first gold-page chunk (0 if none in the top 5).
- **Precision@5**: average share of the top 5 chunks that come from gold pages.

## Limitations

- **Small eval set.** 16 questions (8 per company) is enough to spot gross failures, not to measure small differences. One question changes a per-company score by 0.125.
- **Page-level matching only.** A chunk counts as relevant if it is on a gold page, even if it is a different paragraph of that page. A chunk on a non-gold page that does contain the answer counts as a miss if we did not list that page.
- **Gold pages were found by keyword search** of the extracted PDF text and have not yet been checked by a person (`"verified": false` in `eval_set.json`). Some pages that mention the answer in other words may be missing from the gold lists.
- **Retrieval only.** These numbers say whether the right evidence reaches the LLM; they do not measure whether the generated answer is correct or correctly cited.
- **Precision@5 has a ceiling.** When only one or two pages hold the answer, even perfect retrieval cannot fill all 5 slots with gold pages, so low Precision@5 is expected.
