## Reading the table

- **Cosine, dot product and Euclidean distance rank identically on unit vectors** (top 5 identical for every question), as the formula ||a-b||² = 2 - 2·cos(a,b) predicts. Choosing between them is a matter of convenience, not accuracy.
- **Dot product** equals cosine similarity here because every vector has length 1. With vectors of different lengths it would also reward length, which reflects text properties rather than relevance.
- **Manhattan (L1)** is a different geometry; it is rarely used for sentence embeddings because the models are trained with cosine similarity.
- GreenScope normalizes all vectors and searches by inner product (FAISS `IndexFlatIP`), which equals cosine similarity and matches how the embedding model was trained.
